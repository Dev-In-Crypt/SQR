# HashKey hackathon demo flow

1. Open `https://solidity-scan.com`.
2. In **Network**, select `HashKey Testnet (133)`.
3. In **Review mode**, select `DeFi / PayFi Review Mode`.
4. Submit a verified HashKey contract address.
5. Wait for analysis and open the generated report.
6. Show **Integration readiness** and financial risk sections.
7. Switch between **Builder Report** and **Partner / Investor Report** views.
8. Click **Mint HashKey Testnet receipt** and sign wallet prompts.
9. Open `HashKey Ecosystem Risk Radar` from the top navigation.

Notes:

- The receipt is proof of review artifact existence, not an audit certification.
- HashKey testnet receipt contract: `0x02d42a47cd33f3feefc7cf31b8e29657ed825ab8`.
